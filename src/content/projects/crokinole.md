# Crokinole Board

This is a game board for [Crokinole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crokinole), the greatest thing to come out of Canada since maple syrup.

The board is traditional-style (24 inch diameter, octagonal gutter), which is smaller than a tournament board, both because it would fit in my house, because I was using leftover plywood, and because I didn't want to bother with the circular gutter forming.

The main board area is 3/8 inch plywood, cut with a CNC wood router. The gutters and side rails are pine board, finished with a walnut stain. The whole thing is absolutely covered in layers of polyurethane, then beeswax which is furously rubbed down for smoothness.

Posts come from [Mcmaster Carr](https://www.mcmaster.com/), while the bumpers and discs come from [Crokinole Canada](https://crokinole.ca).

The biggest challenge here was actually getting the rails to fit; cutting precise 67.5 degree angles in 16 boards is somehow harder than it sounds. There's no fancy joinery here, since the big board literally glues everything together.
