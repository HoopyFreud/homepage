# Yellow Sign Dice Box

This is a polyhedral dice box.

The box is made form pine board, assembled as a single piece and then cut to create the lid. Rabbet joints are used throughout, although that's as fancy as I really get with my joinery in general.

The box is laser engraved with a version of the Yellow Sign from [Robert W. Chambers' book The King In Yellow](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/8492). The interior is kaizen foam cut to fit the dice, while the hinges were scavenged from somewhere.

Fun fact, this is my only featured project made entirely with hand tools.
